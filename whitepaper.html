1. Executive Summary

FalconX is a proposed blockchain payment project focused on exploring accessible and efficient digital payment technology. The project is currently under development.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional payment systems can involve multiple intermediaries, processing delays and limited accessibility. FalconX explores blockchain technology as a possible way to improve digital payment experiences.

3. Proposed Solution

FalconX proposes a blockchain-based ecosystem designed around digital payments and transfers. The project will focus on transparency, usability and continued technical development.

4. Blockchain

The proposed FalconX token is planned for the BNB Smart Chain ecosystem. Development and testing should be performed on a suitable test network before any production deployment.
