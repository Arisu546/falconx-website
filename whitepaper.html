🦅 FalconX Pay - Whitepaper v1.0

The Future of Fast, Low-Fee Payments on BNB Chain

1. Vision

FalconX Pay aims to be the fastest payment app in Africa and beyond. Like a falcon, we move fast, cheap, and precise. We want to solve the high fees and slow transactions for everyday users in Libya, Nigeria, and Africa.

2. The Problem

- High gas fees on many networks
- Slow confirmation for small payments
- No simple app for local merchants to accept crypto

3. The Solution - FalconX Pay

A simple mobile-first wallet: Connect MetaMask, Send FLX via QR code, merchant receives instantly on BNB Chain with fees less than $0.01.
